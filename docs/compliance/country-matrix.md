# Privacy and consumer-law matrix

Engineering summary, **not legal advice**. Each row must be confirmed by counsel before launch in that country.

| Region | Law | What the product does | Still needed before launch there |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU / EEA | GDPR | Consent banner (analytics off by default), data export, erasure/anonymisation, retention job, sub-processor list, breach runbook | DPA with each sub-processor; EU representative if no EU entity; translated legal pages |
| UK | UK GDPR / DPA 2018 | As EU | ICO registration; UK representative if needed |
| USA (CA) | CCPA / CPRA | Export and deletion; no sale or sharing of data; analytics opt-in | "Do Not Sell/Share" wording review; notice at collection |
| Brazil | LGPD | Export, erasure, consent record | DPO contact; Portuguese legal pages reviewed |
| Japan | APPI | Purpose statement in privacy policy, export/erasure | Cross-border transfer disclosure |
| Canada | PIPEDA | Consent, access, deletion | Privacy officer named |
| Australia | Privacy Act / APPs | Access, correction, deletion | Notifiable-breach process (see runbook) |
| India | DPDP Act | Consent record, erasure | Consent manager and grievance officer |
| UAE | PDPL | Consent, access, erasure | Cross-border transfer review |
| Kenya | DPA 2019 | Access, erasure | ODPC registration |
| Nigeria | NDPA 2023 | Access, erasure | NDPC registration; DPO |
| Indonesia | PDP Law | Consent, access, erasure | Data-localisation review for payment data |
| Bangladesh | Digital Security / draft data-protection law | Access, erasure | Monitor law changes |

Age: renters must be 18+ (enforced in the driver profile schema). Licence checks are performed before pickup.
Payments: card data never reaches our servers (Stripe Elements, SAQ-A). Payments in this build are simulated.
